civic-connect

CivicConnect

Community platform for reporting municipal issues: potholes, leaks, dumping, streetlights. Includes GPS, photos, status tracking, and analytics for residents and municipal staff.

Effort High Complexity High Monetisation Municipal SaaS / white-label

Affordable citizen engagement and field-ops tooling for municipalities still stuck on phone lines, WhatsApp groups, and paper logs.

Next.js Micronaut / FastAPI PostgreSQL + PostGIS Object storage Docker · GitHub Actions
1

Citizen Issue Reporting

Residents log service failures with evidence and a reference number.

  • Issue categories (pothole, leak, dumping, streetlight…)
  • GPS / map pin
  • Photo & video evidence
  • Description & severity
  • Anonymous or verified submitter
  • Reference / ticket number
2

Maps & GIS

See where problems cluster and route field teams by ward or zone.

  • Interactive map of open issues
  • Ward / suburb boundaries
  • Hotspot heatmaps
  • Nearby issues (dedupe)
  • PostGIS spatial queries
3

Workflow & Assignment

Municipal staff triage, assign, and close issues against SLAs.

  • Status lifecycle (submitted → assigned → in progress → resolved)
  • Department routing
  • Field agent assignment
  • SLA timers & escalation
  • Before / after resolution photos
  • Audit trail
4

Citizen Status & Feedback

Keep reporters informed without calling the switchboard.

  • Track my ticket
  • Push / SMS / email updates
  • Resolution confirmation
  • Satisfaction rating
  • Comment thread (optional)
5

Admin Dashboard & Analytics

Volume, performance, and accountability for managers.

  • Open vs closed by category
  • Average resolution time
  • SLA breach rates
  • Ward leaderboards
  • Export CSV / PDF
  • Role-based access (citizen, agent, admin)
6

Stretch · Smart routing & channels

Optional intelligence and familiar intake channels.

  • Auto-categorise from description
  • Duplicate detection
  • WhatsApp intake bot
  • Offline-friendly mobile capture
edu-attend

EduAttend

School attendance with QR or face recognition, instant parent notifications, and principal dashboards for class and grade trends.

Effort Medium–High Complexity High Monetisation Per-school subscription

Replace paper registers with a practical attendance and parent-comms stack for South African schools.

React Flutter FastAPI PostgreSQL OpenCV Docker
1

Teacher Attendance UI

Mark a class present, late, or absent in minutes.

  • Class & period selection
  • Manual roll call
  • QR scan check-in
  • Bulk mark present
  • Late / excused reasons
  • Same-day corrections
2

QR & Face Recognition

Optional faster capture at the gate or classroom door.

  • Learner QR badges
  • Camera check-in
  • Face enrolment (opt-in)
  • Confidence thresholds
  • Fallback to manual mark
3

Parent Portal / Mobile

Caregivers see attendance without calling the school office.

  • Linked learners
  • Daily attendance status
  • Absence history
  • Excuse / sick notes
  • Push notifications
4

Principal Dashboard

School-wide visibility for leadership and interventions.

  • Attendance % by grade
  • Chronic absentee alerts
  • Class comparison
  • Day-of-week trends
  • Export for district reporting
5

Notifications & Reporting

Close the loop when a learner is marked absent or late.

  • Instant absent alerts
  • Late arrival alerts
  • Weekly parent summary
  • SMS / email / push channels
  • Term attendance certificates
6

School Admin & Security

Learners, classes, staff roles, and privacy controls.

Admin

  • Learner & guardian profiles
  • Timetable / periods
  • Teacher assignments

Security

  • Role-based access
  • Consent for biometrics
  • Audit logs

KasiMarket

Marketplace and ops toolkit for township businesses: catalogues, ordering, delivery, and analytics, with a Pro path toward POS, inventory, and loyalty.

Effort Medium Complexity Medium Monetisation Fees · subscriptions · promos

An affordable cloud business platform for South African SMEs, informal traders, and township entrepreneurs.

Next.js NestJS PostgreSQL Redis Docker

Customer Marketplace

Discover nearby shops and order without leaving the neighbourhood.

  • Browse products
  • Search nearby businesses
  • Order online
  • Reserve stock
  • Click & Collect
  • Delivery requests
  • Ratings & reviews
2

Merchant Portal

Publish catalogues and manage day-to-day shop data.

  • Products & categories
  • Pricing & discounts
  • Stock levels
  • Business profile & hours
  • Order inbox
  • Promoted listings
3

Orders & Payments

From cart to fulfilment with local payment patterns in mind.

  • Cart & checkout

  • Order status tracking
  • Refunds & cancellations
  • Delivery driver assignment
4

Inventory & POS (Pro)

Counter sales and stock control for merchants who want more than a storefront.

  • Barcode / QR scan
  • Low stock alerts
  • Purchase orders

  • Daily cash-up
5

Customers & Loyalty

Know regulars and reward repeat spend.

  • Customer profiles

  • Loyalty points
  • Promotions
  • Birthday rewards
6

Analytics, Reports & Alerts

What sold, what stalled, and what needs restocking.

Insights

  • Daily / weekly / monthly sales
  • Best sellers
  • Slow-moving stock
  • Revenue & profit

Exports & alerts

  • PDF / Excel / CSV

Stretch · AI assistants

Optional intelligence on top of operational data.

React Micronaut PostgreSQL FreeRADIUS MikroTik API Docker

Subscriber Portal

Self-serve account, packages, and top-ups for end users.

  • Account registration
  • View active package
  • Data / time remaining
  • Top-up / renew
  • Payment history
  • Support tickets
2

Captive Portal

Onboard devices when they join the Wi-Fi network.

  • Login / voucher redeem
  • Package selection
  • Terms acceptance
  • Session start / expiry
  • Branded splash pages
3

Admin & Network Ops

Operators manage sites, APs, and subscriber lifecycle.

  • Site / hotspot inventory
  • Subscriber CRUD
  • Suspend / reinstate
  • Device MAC binding
  • MikroTik / RADIUS sync
  • Outage notes
4

Billing & Packages

Vouchers, recurring plans, and clear money trails.

  • Bandwidth profiles
  • Time-based vouchers
  • Data-cap packages
  • Monthly subscriptions
  • Payment recording
  • Invoice / receipt export
5

Usage Monitoring & Reporting

See who is online, what is congested, and what is earning.

  • Active sessions
  • Bandwidth utilisation
  • Top consumers
  • Revenue by package
  • Churn / expiry alerts
  • Export reports
6

Stretch · Automation & alerts

Reduce manual voucher and support work.

  • Auto-suspend on expiry

  • SMS payment reminders
  • Agent commission tracking
clinic-queue

ClinicQueue

Digital clinic appointments and queue management with patient notifications and analytics: fewer crowded waiting rooms, clearer flow for staff.

Effort Medium Complexity Medium Monetisation Clinic license / hosted SaaS

A low-cost queue layer for South African primary healthcare facilities still running on paper tickets and verbal call-outs.

Flutter React FastAPI PostgreSQL Redis Docker
1

Patient App

Book ahead or join today's queue from a phone.

  • Find nearby clinics
  • Book appointments
  • Join walk-in queue
  • Ticket / queue number
  • Estimated wait time
  • Cancel / reschedule
2

Live Queue & Check-in

Reception controls the floor without shouting numbers into a crowd.

  • Check-in at reception / kiosk
  • Call next patient
  • Skip / no-show handling
  • Priority / emergency lanes
  • Service type queues (pharmacy, vitals, doctor)
  • Display board support
3

Clinic Dashboard

Staff see load, slots, and who is waiting right now.

  • Today's appointments
  • Live waiting list
  • Room / clinician assignment
  • Slot capacity settings
  • Patient search
  • Staff roles (reception, nurse, admin)
4

Notifications

Tell patients when to leave home, not hours early.

  • Booking confirmations
  • "You're next" alerts
  • Delay / clinic closed notices
  • SMS / push reminders
  • Missed appointment follow-up
5

Reporting & Analytics

Throughput and wait times for facility managers.

  • Average wait time
  • Patients served per day
  • No-show rates
  • Peak hour heatmaps
  • Service-type breakdown
  • Export CSV / PDF
6

Stretch · Continuity of care

Light extras that stay within a realistic MVP-plus scope.

  • Chronic patient recall lists
  • Multi-clinic / district view
  • WhatsApp status bot
  • Anonymous feedback after visit
isango-pass

IsangoPass

Access control and visitor management for offices, campuses, estates, and industrial sites: pre-registration, check-in, badges, host notifications, and audit-ready entry logs.

Effort Medium–High Complexity Medium–High Monetisation Site license / hosted SaaS

Replace paper visitor books and ad-hoc WhatsApp gate lists with a secure, auditable access platform for South African workplaces and residential estates.

React / Next.js Flutter (kiosk / guard) FastAPI / Micronaut PostgreSQL Redis Docker
1

Visitor Pre-Registration

Hosts invite visitors ahead of time; visitors arrive expected, not as surprises.

  • Host-created invitations
  • Visitor self-registration link
  • Expected date / time window
  • Purpose of visit
  • ID / vehicle details capture
  • Approval workflow (optional)
2

Gate / Reception Check-in

Guards and receptionists process arrivals quickly with a clear queue of expected guests.

  • Walk-in registration
  • QR / invite code scan
  • ID verification checklist
  • Photo capture at gate
  • Badge / pass printing
  • Sign-out / exit capture
3

Access Zones & Credentials

Control who can enter which areas, and for how long.

  • Buildings / floors / zones
  • Time-bound access windows
  • Staff access cards / PINs
  • Visitor temporary credentials
  • Blacklist / watchlist
  • Emergency lockdown flag
4

Host & Guard Notifications

Tell hosts when their guest arrives; alert security when something is off.

  • "Visitor has arrived" alerts
  • Overstay / no sign-out reminders
  • Denied-entry notifications
  • SMS / push / email channels
  • Escort required flags
5

Employee & Contractor Access

Ongoing access for staff and contractors, not only one-off visitors.

People

  • Employee directory sync
  • Contractor companies
  • Department / role mapping

Lifecycle

  • Onboarding / offboarding
  • Access expiry dates
  • Lost-card revoke
6

Admin Dashboard & Audit Logs

Who entered, when, where: ready for compliance and incident review.

  • Live on-site visitor board
  • Entry / exit history
  • Peak arrival times
  • Denied attempts report
  • Export CSV / PDF
  • Role-based admin (guard, host, security manager)
7

Stretch · Hardware & smart access

Optional integrations beyond a software-only MVP.

  • Turnstile / door controller hooks
  • ANPR number-plate recognition
  • Face / biometric check-in (opt-in)
  • Multi-site estate / campus view
