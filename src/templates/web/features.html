Features

What {{ app_name }} does

One queue per clinic room, four ways to join it, and a waiting-room screen that counts it down. Everything below is specified with acceptance criteria; the screenshots on the front door are illustrative and carry no real patient data.

See the screens Why it's different
{# ── The four doors ───────────────────────────────────────── #}
Four doors, one queue

However you join, you take the same place in line

The channel is only a way in. A ticket taken over USSD on a twenty-year-old handset sits in exactly the same queue, in exactly the same order, as one taken in the app.

📱App & web

An installable PWA and a plain web page: search, join, and watch your position move in real time.

☎️USSD, no data

A numbered menu on any handset. No smartphone, no airtime for data, no app to install.

💬WhatsApp

A short conversation that ends in a ticket number, on the app most people already have open.

🧾Walk in

Reception adds you in about ten seconds, or you scan the QR at the door yourself.

{# ── Patients ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
For patients

Hold your place without holding the wall up

A morning off work should not be the price of a twenty-minute consultation. Join before you leave home, and wait where you would rather wait.

📍Clinic discovery

Radius search over PostGIS with a public / private / all toggle, live queue length, open-now status, and both a map and a list.

🎟️Join from anywhere

A ticket number and a live position before you leave home, from the app, USSD, WhatsApp or the web.

⏱️An honest estimate

Your wait is projected from how fast the room is actually moving today, and it updates as it changes.

🔔Notifications that arrive

Push, SMS or WhatsApp, with quiet hours, opt-outs, and hard per-clinic cost caps so a clinic can never be surprised by a bill.

🗓️Scheduled appointments

Booked slots that convert into a live ticket automatically at the right moment, with a reminder the day before.

🚗Virtual waiting room

Wait nearby instead of on a bench. You are called forward accounting for the travel time you said you need.

👨‍👩‍👧Book for a dependant

One household, one smartphone: a parent for a child, a daughter for her mother — with the consent recorded.

💊Chronic medication reminders

Recurring collection nudges with a one-keypress join, for the queue you have to stand in every month.

🌍Five languages

English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans and Sesotho — across menus, messages and the spoken call-out.

{# ── Staff ────────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
For clinic staff

The whole line, from one screen

Built for a front desk on a Monday morning: one very large button, no training day, and a screen that tells the truth when the connection drops.

🖥️One dashboard

Every active queue live, with one very large Call Next and an honest offline state rather than a stale one.

🚶Walk-ins in ten seconds

The same sequence as a remote join, into the same fair queue, with a printable stub and an optional SMS.

🏥Multi-room queues

Triage → doctor → pharmacy, with transfers that keep the visit intact instead of starting it over.

⚖️Audited priority overrides

Bump a visibly unwell patient in two taps — with a reason code and a permanent trail, so a queue jump is never invisible.

📺Waiting-room board

Number, time, privacy-gated name, an audio call-out, and unattended recovery after load-shedding.

⚙️Self-service settings

Hours, holidays, closures, queues, services, staff and display mode. No developer, no support ticket.

📊Reports that sell themselves

Wait times, no-show rate, channel mix and a busiest-hour heatmap — the numbers a manager takes to their district.

💬Post-visit feedback

One question, one tap: a service-quality number that means something because everyone answers it.

{# ── Platform ─────────────────────────────────────────────── #}
Platform, privacy & compliance

Privacy is the default, not a setting someone remembers

A queue in a clinic is health information the moment it carries a name. Every new clinic starts number-only, and every widening of that is a recorded, revocable choice.

🔐RBAC & multi-tenancy

Roles and scope tiers an administrator edits in the console. A clinic can never read another clinic's data, and cross-site access returns 404, not 403.

🛡️Privacy by default

Every new clinic starts number-only on the board. Names and reasons are opt-in, consent-gated and audited.

🧹Retention & purge

Reason text and visit notes are deleted automatically after a short window — with a test that proves they are gone.

📜Tamper-evident audit

A hash-chained, append-only log: a retrospective edit is detectable rather than merely discouraged.

📤Access & erasure

A patient can ask what is held about them and have it deleted. POPIA rights, built rather than promised.

🏛️District dashboards

Anonymised area-level wait trends with small-cell suppression, for health-department planning.

🔌Offline & load-shedding

The board recovers unattended and the dashboard says so out loud when it is stale, because the power will go out.

♿Accessibility

Large type, high contrast, audio call-outs and keyboard operation — a waiting room is not a design showcase.

Privacy modes on the board. A clinic chooses one of three: number only, number plus a first name and last initial, or number plus full name. The first is the default, the other two require recorded patient consent, and the reason a patient is here is never displayed. Read the privacy notice for what is held, why, and for how long.
