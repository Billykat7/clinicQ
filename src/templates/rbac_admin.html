                Nav gates

                Re-point an already-shipped sidebar icon or console tab to a different resource, verb or named action, and set how wide a grant it needs (its scope tier), with no deploy needed. A surface's label/icon/position stays code-declared; only which permission it checks, and how far that permission must reach, are editable here. Business marks a whole-business back-office surface: a caller whose grant on that resource is scoped to their own rows is refused. Own marks a surface that is itself a first-person view, so either tier opens it. Leave the tier blank to keep the current one.
