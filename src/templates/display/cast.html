{#- The Cast receiver ClinicQ registers with Google (Issue 237). This is the page a Chromecast loads, and the *only* page it will load: a Chromecast runs an application, an application is a URL registered at cast.google.com/publish under an id, and a sender may launch a registered id and nothing else. So the clinic cannot be named in the address — it arrives afterwards, as a message over the Cast connection carrying a one-time claim code (src/modules/display/casting.py), which cast-receiver.js spends at /display/claim. Deliberately not extending layouts/board.html: this page frames the board rather than being it, and it carries a policy of its own (see below), so it owns its whole document. **Its own Content-Security-Policy.** A receiver that does not load Google's receiver SDK is not a receiver, and that SDK is served only from www.gstatic.com — it cannot be vendored under /static the way Leaflet was. Writing the exception here, on the one page that needs it, is what keeps it off every other page's policy: src/core/security_headers.py leaves a policy a handler already set alone. Everything else stays shut — 'self' for styles, images, fonts and connections, and no object, base or form target at all. -#} {{ app_name }} · Waiting-room board {#- The splash a TV shows between "ClinicQ opened" and "the board arrived".
