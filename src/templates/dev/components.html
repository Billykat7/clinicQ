Components

Roles, not colours: each swatch is filled from its token, so switching the theme changes every swatch and every screen together.

Type scale

A layout chooses which step its text reads: the console sits low on the ladder, the patient's ticket number near the top and the board at --step-5.

Toast

A short message in one live region. Raised from the page (the toast() macro), from an htmx response (toast_trigger(), below) or from a button, as here, with no script of its own.
